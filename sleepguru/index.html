Guru Help Center

Guru (Sleep Guru) is published by Pas à Pas Studio LLC.

What is Guru?

Guru builds a personalized wind-down routine to help you relax, fall asleep, and stay asleep. Wind down with short stories, follow guided breathing to slow your pace, and listen to calming music and soundscapes.

Guru Unlimited subscription

Full access to Guru requires a Guru Unlimited subscription. The price and billing period are shown in the app before you confirm, in your local currency. Some plans start with a free trial.

  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Any unused part of a free trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To cancel on your iPhone:

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap your name at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Select Guru.
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription.

Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

Refunds

Apple processes all payments, so refund requests go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't issue refunds directly, but we're happy to help if you get stuck.

I subscribed but Guru asks me to subscribe again

Open the subscription screen in Guru and tap Restore. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe. If it still doesn't work, email us with the date of purchase.

Why does Guru ask for the microphone?

Some breathing exercises listen to your breath so Guru can follow your pace. The sound is analyzed on your device as you breathe and is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. You can skip these exercises or turn off microphone access at any time in Settings → Guru.
